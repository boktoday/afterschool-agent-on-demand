## The onetoeight programme

This landing page is for the **Afterschool Agent** programme — on-demand training
where teens create their own Hermes agent to learn the eight essentials:

1. **Silence & Stillness** — being still without your phone
2. **Body as Temple** — taking care of the only vehicle you get
3. **Scripture & Wisdom** — tested convictions from your tradition
4. **Nature Connection** — going outside, under sky, in air
5. **Digital Self-Defence** — knowing what you give away when you click "agree"
6. **Life Skills** — feeding, fixing, building things
7. **Real Community** — people who'd call you at 2am
8. **Tested Character** — beliefs you've actually examined

The full guide is at [https://onetoeight.org](https://onetoeight.org).
